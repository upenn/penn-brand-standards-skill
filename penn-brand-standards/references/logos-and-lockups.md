# Penn Logos, Lockups, and Restricted Symbols

Source: Penn Brand Guidelines (updated Aug 13, 2025), pp. 4–17, plus the logo download page. Official downloads: https://branding.web-resources.upenn.edu/logos-and-branding/download-penn-logos

## Logo anatomy

The main Penn logo combines three elements:
1. **University Shield** (coat of arms, adopted 1933)
2. **Penn Mark** (rendered in Perpetua)
3. **"University of Pennsylvania" logotype** (rendered in ITC Stone Serif)

The primary horizontal lockup is the default. Alternate lockups exist only for situations the primary cannot accommodate.

## Available variants (official files)

| Variant | When to use |
|---|---|
| Logo with Shield and Logotype (standard) | Default for light backgrounds |
| Same, reversed | Dark/blue backgrounds |
| Penn Logo with Shield (no logotype) | Tight spaces where logotype becomes illegible |
| Shield only | Decorative uses where the full logo is inappropriate |
| Vertical stacked logo | Only when horizontal space can't fit the minimum logo width |
| Simplified Shield | Digital-only, small formats (favicons) and wayfinding; **requires permission** — ucomms-web@upenn.edu |

Each variant ships as EPS (RGB for digital, CMYK for print), PNG, and JPG. Illustrator (.ai) files available on request. Older black & white / blue monotone versions: contact University Communications.

School logos (for Penn's 12 schools) come from each school's communications office — never rebuild them. The Penn Athletics "Split P" comes via Athletics communications.

## Sizing and spacing

- **Minimum width is determined by legibility** — there is no fixed numeric minimum for print or digital. Judge it: if the logotype is hard to read, the logo is too small; step down to a simplified variant.
- **No maximum size.**
- **Clear space**: the area around the logo must stay free of all other elements (text, images, page edges) so nothing detracts from it.
- **Safe zone**: the internal padding area where the logo is allowed air and where you may align other elements.

## Do's and Don'ts (official incorrect-usage list)

Do:
- Use official renditions only; replace outdated logo iterations
- Use approved alternative lockups when the primary doesn't fit
- Choose RGB files for screens, CMYK files for print

Don't:
- Discolor the logo (only full red/blue, black, white, or Penn blue)
- Change the proportions
- Stretch or distort
- Change the positioning of logo parts
- Separate or use shield elements on their own
- Use the Penn Mark on its own
- Overlay on patterned backgrounds that render it illegible
- Redraw, reconstruct, or modify the Shield in any way

## Logo variations for centers, departments, and divisions

Departments don't get custom logos — they extend the Penn identity system with approved lockup patterns. The guide's baseline rules:

- Department name goes **beneath** or **to the right** of the Penn Mark (or of the full logo)
- The Shield and Penn Mark remain **intact and unaltered**
- A vertical dividing line (when used) matches the width and color of the University of Pennsylvania logotype
- Typeface, kerning, and spacing stay consistent with the central identity (ITC Stone Serif)
- The lockup forms a neat, confined, approximately rectangular area
- Use proportions to reinforce the relationship between University and department
- Consider formal vs. informal contexts when choosing the lockup

Pattern options from the guide: (FIG 1) Penn Mark replaced by department name, shield and University name unchanged; (FIG 2) department name below the logo; (FIG 3) department name to the right of the logo.

**Centers, departments, and divisions may not create their own identity systems.** Logos approved under pre-2020 guidelines should be revised to this format as soon as practicable. Limited exceptions for cultural organizations with unique external-audience missions may incorporate Penn colors, insignia, name, or font — but must not compromise brand integrity.

## School logos

- The 12 schools each have approved lockups: school name + separation line + University of Pennsylvania logotype.
- All school logos **must** include the University of Pennsylvania logotype. No exceptions or modifications.
- Main school logos are required in the most prominent places: promotional publications, school/department websites, business cards, letterhead.
- **Simplified school logos** (University logotype and dividing line removed, school name expanded to shield height) are for informal uses — website footers, internal newsletters, social media, merchandise — or small sizes where the University of Pennsylvania mark would be illegible. Shield and Penn Mark still remain intact.
- Penn Carey Law: short form "Penn Carey Law"; long form "University of Pennsylvania Carey Law School" (since Sept 1, 2022).
- School department lockups follow the same pattern as University department lockups, anchored on the school name.

## Joint program logos

- Equal weight to participating schools
- Program name above or to the right of the schools, forming a neat rectangular unit
- Secondary colors may be added **only** if they adhere to approved values
- Shield and Penn Mark remain intact

## Co-branding with non-Penn logos (pre-existing logos)

- Non-Penn logo beneath or to the right of the school/University name
- Equal sizing for both logos
- Shield and Penn Mark remain intact
- Same dividing-line, spacing, and rectangular-area rules

## Restricted symbols

| Symbol | Restriction |
|---|---|
| University Seal | Only for authenticating signatures on documents issued/authorized by the Trustees. No other use. |
| Shield with flourish | Ornamental/decorative uses; permission required from the Office of the University Secretary |
| Shield with banner (Latin motto *Leges Sine Moribus Vanae* — "Laws without morals are useless") | Ornamental/decorative uses; permission required |
| Simplified Shield | Small digital formats and wayfinding only; permission from ucomms-web@upenn.edu |
| Split P | Penn Athletics and spirit uses only — never for school or administrative purposes |
