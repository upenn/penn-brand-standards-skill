# Penn Stationery and Print Specs

Source: Penn Brand Guidelines pp. 20–25, 33–37. Penn uses a preferred vendor for printing (stationery details: https://cms.business-services.upenn.edu/publications/04-stationery-and-business-cards.html). Use these specs when designing print-ready stationery or documents that mimic official stationery.

Universal stationery rules:

- All pieces carry the **official logo printed in red and blue**
- School/departmental/personal information prints in **Penn blue only**
- Type is **ITC Stone Serif** (licensed; MyFonts)
- Business cards: 80-pound white cover stock with a laid finish
- Letterhead: 24-pound recycled white bond with a laser-compatible laid finish, milled specially for the University
- Correspondence uses the **modified block letter** style

**What the guidelines deliberately don't specify** (don't invent values): no numeric logo size or placement coordinates on stationery (follow the specimen layouts in the guidelines PDF), no bleed/trim tolerances, and no spot-vs-process preference beyond the PMS/CMYK values being equivalent. For production, request the official templates/artwork from the preferred vendor or University Communications rather than measuring from the spec sheet.

## Business cards

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 3.5" × 2" |
| Margins | 0.187" all sides |
| Name/title | ITC Stone Serif, 7.5 pt / 7.5 pt leading |
| Address block | ITC Stone Serif, 6.5 pt / 7.5 pt leading |
| Layout | Logo top; department or division block below-left; name/title block right; logotype centered in the width of the card |

Address block contents, in order: Department or Division / Department Two / Room Number & Building Name / Street Address / Philadelphia, PA 19104-#### / Tel … Fax … / email / www.upenn.edu/websiteaddress

## Letterhead

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Sizes | 8.5" × 11" (large) and 5.5" × 8.5" (small) |
| Margins | 0.5" all sides |
| Department block | ITC Stone Serif 9 pt / 11.5 leading (primary), 8 pt / 10 leading (secondary lines) |
| Name/title block (right) | ITC Stone Serif 9 pt / 11.5 leading; title in ITC Stone Serif Italic 8 pt / 11.5 leading |
| Footer contact line | ITC Stone Serif 8 pt, centered in the width of the letterhead |

Layout: logo aligned left ½" from edge; department block left; name and title aligned right ½" from edge; contact strip (address • tel • fax • email • www) centered at the foot.

### Letter format (modified block)

- Date typed near the center of the page
- Inside address in block style
- Paragraphs block or indented, double-spaced between paragraphs
- Attention line (if any) between inside address and salutation, double-spaced above and below
- Complimentary close two lines below the body, near center; signature aligned with the close
- Page 2 headers align with the name/title block; 1.25" top margin on continuation pages

## Envelopes

All envelopes: official logo in red and blue; department info in blue only. Return-address type: ITC Stone Serif 9 pt / 11 leading (name/department), 7.5 pt / 10 leading (address lines). Envelope return address margins: 0.312" from left and top edges.

| Envelope | Size |
|---|---|
| #10 (large letterhead) | 4.125" × 9.5" |
| #10 window | 4.125" × 9.5" |
| #6-3/4 (small letterhead) | 3.625" × 6.5" |
| #9 reply | 4.875" × 8.875" |

## Print color conversions

- Penn Red: CMYK 24, 100, 100, 25 · PMS 201
- Penn Blue: CMYK 100, 93, 32, 32 · PMS 288
- The Brand Guidelines PDF itself is set in RGB for on-screen viewing — when producing print material, CMYK values are mandatory.

## School stationery

Same specs as above; school name replaces the department block, and school lockups (with the University of Pennsylvania logotype) are required on business cards and letterhead. Schools with approved logos print their lockup; administrative offices and departments without logos use the standard Penn logo + ITC Stone Serif text treatment.
